Notes that the table cannot carry:

- **Open CASCADE** is LGPL-2.1 **with the Open CASCADE Exception**, which vcpkg records
  only as `LGPL-2.1-only`. The exception permits linking into applications that are not
  themselves LGPL, subject to conditions. OCCT is confined to the separately replaceable
  `openmcad_occt.dll` (ADR-0003) so those conditions stay trivially satisfiable: the
  library can be replaced without rebuilding the application.
- **FreeType** is dual licensed, `FTL OR GPL-2.0-or-later`. OpenMCAD takes it under the
  FreeType Licence, which requires acknowledgement in the documentation. That
  acknowledgement is this entry.
- FreeType and its dependencies (libpng, brotli, bzip2, zlib) arrive transitively through
  OCCT's visualisation modules, which are themselves pulled in by the STEP exchange
  module. Nothing in Phase 1 renders text. Trimming the closure would shrink both the
  payload and the licence surface, and is worth doing before first release.
